1. The v2026.0.0 Global Seismic Vulnerability Model. [[GitHub](https://github.com/gem/global_vulnerability_model/tree/v2026.0.0)][[Docs](https://docs.openquake.org/vulnerability/)][[Request Full Model](https://www.globalquakemodel.org/license-request/global-vulnerability-model)]
